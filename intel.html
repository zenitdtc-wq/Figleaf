🌐 Command & Control Traffic Indicators

ZeroDayRAT, AhMyth, and Anubis all use Firebase or Telegram as their C2. Here's how to detect that traffic on your device.

Firebase C2 Fingerprint
  • Domain: *.firebaseio.com
  • Domain: *.firebase.google.com
  • Port: 443 (HTTPS), persistent long-polling connections
  • Used by: ZeroDayRAT, AhMyth, Anubis (2025–2026 variants)
  • Red flag: Firebase traffic from an app that has no visible cloud feature
Telegram Bot C2 Fingerprint
  • Domain: api.telegram.org
  • Polling endpoint: /bot[token]/getUpdates
  • Used by: ZeroDayRAT, AsyncRAT variants, several banking trojans
  • Red flag: Telegram API traffic from a non-messaging app, especially at night
How to check on your device
  • Android: Settings → Network → Data Usage → sort apps by Mobile Data. Look for spikes in apps that shouldn't need the internet
  • iOS: Settings → Mobile Data → scroll to app list, check unusual data consumers
  • Advanced: Use NetGuard (Android, free) or LittleSnitch (iOS) to see per-app traffic in real time
  • Any app sending data regularly at 2–5 minute intervals while idle = strong spyware indicator
