The 4 major threats

How phones get hacked

You don't need to click a bad link to be compromised. Here's what ShieldScan watches for.

🕵️
Spyware & Stalkerware
Hidden apps that silently track your location, read your messages, record calls, and drain your battery.
Battery draining faster than usual
Phone warm when idle
Unknown apps in your list
Microphone / camera active unexpectedly
🎣
Phishing & Fake Apps
Fraudulent links in texts and emails that clone real login pages to steal your username, password, and payment details.
Unexpected login alerts from real services
Apps asking for unusual permissions
Password stopped working suddenly
Texts with urgent links from unknown numbers
📡
Public Wi-Fi Attacks
On unsecured networks, attackers position themselves between you and the internet to intercept your data in real time.
Connected to open / unsaved Wi-Fi
No VPN active on public networks
Strange browser certificate warnings
Slow, intermittent connection in public places
📲
SIM Swap & Account Takeover
Attackers convince your carrier to transfer your phone number, then use it to bypass two-factor authentication on your accounts.
Sudden loss of mobile signal with no reason
Can't receive calls or texts
Unexpected 2FA codes you didn't request
Accounts locked without your action
